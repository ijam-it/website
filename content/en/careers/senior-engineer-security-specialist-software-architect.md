We are looking for a senior professional with strong experience in security, software development, platform engineering or cloud-native delivery.

You do not have to fit into a single box. We are looking for people who understand complex environments, take ownership and help clients with solutions that are technically sound and practically executable.

## You bring

- broad experience in software, security, cloud or platform engineering;
- the ability to make complex challenges clear;
- experience with architecture, delivery or technical leadership;
- attention to quality, risk and maintainability;
- strong communication skills;
- the independence to help clients and teams move forward.

## What you will do

- design and improve secure digital solutions;
- advise on architecture, security and delivery;
- guide teams and technical decisions;
- build platforms, applications or security capabilities;
- contribute to the development of iJam, xsGrant and Opsteady.
