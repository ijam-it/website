We are looking for a frontend designer with strong UX skills who can translate design into working, accessible and well-considered interfaces.

You will work on digital products where usability, reliability and clarity matter. Think portals, dashboards, workflows, forms and applications for professional users.

## You bring

- experience with UX design and frontend development
- a strong sense of simplicity, structure and user flow
- experience with design systems or component-based design
- attention to accessibility and responsive design
- the ability to work with users, engineers and stakeholders
- the drive to make complex processes understandable

## What you will do

- design user flows, wireframes and interfaces
- build or guide frontend implementations
- improve existing digital products
- collaborate with engineers, product owners and clients
- contribute to design quality within iJam® and our labels
