iJam helps organisations design, build and improve secure digital solutions. We work at the intersection of software, security, cloud, platform engineering and organisational development.

## Platform Engineering

We design and build internal platforms that help development teams deliver faster, safer and more independently. This includes Kubernetes, cloud platforms, CI/CD, developer experience, observability and self-service capabilities.

## Cloud & Kubernetes

We help organisations with cloud strategy, migration, architecture and platform decisions. From assessment to design, and from implementation to team guidance.

## Secure Software Delivery

We bring security closer to the development process. Not as a delay, but as part of modern software delivery. This includes secure CI/CD, threat modeling, dependency management, vulnerability management and security by design.

## Security Architecture & Monitoring

We have deep experience in security architecture, monitoring, SIEM, threat intelligence, detection engineering, SOC improvements and incident response processes.

## Digital Transformation & Team Building

We help organisations structure teams, processes and ways of working so digital change becomes sustainable. From initial setup and leadership to coaching, agile delivery and DevOps adoption.

## Software Architecture & Development

We design and build custom applications, portals, integrations, microservices and MVPs. Always with attention to maintainability, security and production use.
