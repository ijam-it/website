Maarten builds and develops teams, departments and programmes in complex organisations. He brings structure, energy and direction to environments where technology, organisation, procurement, processes and people come together.

His experience includes building DevOps and software development teams, leading multidisciplinary security teams, public procurement, supplier management, agile transformation and service delivery. He has worked on new development departments, national detection capabilities, procurement of threat information, rollout of security solutions, integrations between security systems and large-scale change programmes across government, education, lottery and public services.
