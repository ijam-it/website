Richard helps organisations improve digital resilience through security architecture, monitoring services and technical delivery. He moves between product ownership, technical design, integrations and operational security in complex environments.

His experience includes registration and reporting processes, vulnerability management, workplace security, security monitoring, detection, threat information and security automation. He has worked on digital services, integrations between security systems, standardisation of information exchange and securing large government, financial and telecom environments.
